# Positional Play (Juego de Posición) — Framework Reference

## Core Principles

Positional Play is a tactical philosophy emphasizing structured spacing, coordinated movement, and positional superiority over individual duels. Based primarily on Pep Guardiola's methodology as documented in *Pep Confidential* (Perarnau, 2014).

### Four Zones of the Pitch

Divide the pitch vertically into four zones:

| Zone | Purpose | Key Actions |
|------|---------|-------------|
| **Build-up Zone** (Defense + defensive midfield) | Structured possession from the back | Center-backs split, fullbacks width, #6 drops to receive |
| **Creation Zone** (Midfield) | Progression through combinations | #8/#10s occupy half-spaces, #6 dictates tempo |
| **Final Third** (Attacking midfield + attack) | Chance creation | Wingers touch the line, #9 makes runs behind, #10 arrives late |
| **Finishing Zone** (Penalty area) | Conversion | Spacing for crosses, cut-backs, and second-phase shots |

### Horizontal Sectors

Each vertical zone has three horizontal sectors:

1. **Wide Channels** (5m from each touchline) — Wingers and fullbacks occupy
2. **Half-Spaces** (between wide channels and center) — #8s, #10s, and drifting forwards
3. **Central Channel** (middle 30m) — #6, #9, and dropping midfielders

## Formation Templates

### 4-3-3 (Classic Positional Play)

**Build-up Shape:**
```
GK
  4   2   3   5
      6
  8      10
7           9      11
```

**Key Relationships:**
- **2-3-6 Triangle:** Right side build-up (RB, RCB, #6)
- **3-6-8 Box:** Right side progression (RB, #6, #8)
- **4-5-11 Triangle:** Left side progression (LB, #10, LW)
- **9-10-11 Front Three:** Final third coordination

**Pressing Triggers:**
- **First Press:** #9 presses CB, #7/#11 press CBs facing their own goal
- **Coverage:** #8/#10 shadow #6, #6 screens passes into pivot
- **Compactness:** Back four step up to compress the mid-block

### 3-5-2 (Hybrid System)

**Build-up Shape:**
```
GK
  2   4   5
3            6            8
  7   9     10     11
```

**Key Relationships:**
- **Back Three:** Width from #3/#5, #4 as libero
- **Mid Five:** Box midfield with #6 as pivot
- **Front Two:** #9 occupies CBs, #10 drops to connect

**Transitions:**
- **Defensive:** #7/#11 drop to make back five
- **Offensive:** #3/#5 become wing-backs, #7/#11 pinch inside

## Structural Superiority

### Numerical Superiority

Create 3v2 or 4v3 situations in specific zones:

- **Build-up:** 3v2 against first press (GK + 2 CBs vs 2 forwards)
- **Midfield:** 4v3 through pivot + two #8s vs 3 opposing midfielders
- **Wide Areas:** 2v1 with fullback + winger vs opposition fullback

**Operational Principle:** Always have a free player for the pass.

### Positional Superiority

Position players between opposition lines:

- **#6:** Between defense and midfield (receives facing forward)
- **#8s:** In half-spaces (cannot be pressed by both CB and fullback)
- **#10:** Between midfield and defense (arrives late into finishing zone)

### Qualitative Superiority

Create favorable matchups:

- **Technical:** Put your best dribbler 1v1 against weak defender
- **Physical:** Target aerial duels for #9 against smaller CBs
- **Tactical:** Exploit defenders uncomfortable being dragged wide

## Progressive Relationships

### Passing Combinations

1. **Third-Man Run:** Player A passes to B, C runs behind, B plays through-ball
   - Example: #6 → #10 (dropping), #8 runs beyond, #10 plays through

2. **Up-Back-Through:** A plays forward to B, B plays back to A, A plays through to C
   - Example: #4 → #2, #2 → #4, #4 → #10 arriving

3. **Overlap:** Inside pass + fullback overlap
   - Example: #7 drops to receive, #3 overlaps, #7 plays through

4. **Inverted Run:** Wide player cuts inside, fullback provides width
   - Example: #11 inverts, #5 overlaps, #11 receives in half-space

### Movement Triggers

**When #6 receives facing forward:**
- Both #8s make diagonal runs into half-spaces
- #9 checks to the near post, then spins in behind
- #10 delays run to receive lay-off

**When fullback receives wide:**
- Nearest #8 makes near-post run
- Winger makes far-post run
- #6 offers safety valve at top of box

**When winger receives in final third:**
- Fullback overlaps for width
- #9 makes near-post run
- #10 makes far-post, delayed run
- Opposite #8 arrives at edge of box for second phase

## Defensive Organization

### Pressing Zones

| Zone | Press Intensity | Key Pressers | Coverage |
|------|-----------------|--------------|----------|
| **Opposition Build-up** | High | #9, #7, #11 | #8/#10 shadow #6, #6 screens pivot |
| **Middle Third** | Medium | Front three drop, #8s engage | Form compact mid-block |
| **Defensive Third** | Low | Back four in box | Protect penalty area |

### Counter-Pressing

**When Possession is Lost:**
1. **Nearest Player:** Immediate press (0-2 seconds)
2. **Supporting Players:** Cut passing lanes (2-4 seconds)
3. **Distant Players:** Recover defensive shape (4+ seconds)

**Critical Zones for Counter-Press:**
- After losing possession in opponent's half
- After unsuccessful through-ball
- After losing aerial duel in final third

### Block Shapes

- **High Block (45-60m from goal):** When pressing high
- **Mid-Block (35-45m):** Default defensive shape
- **Low Block (25-35m):** When protecting lead or tired

## Adaptation Guidelines

### Against Low Block

1. **Patience:** Circulate possession until opponent's block shifts
2. **Width:** Fullbacks push high to stretch defensive block
3. **Diagonal Runs:** #8s make runs from deep to disorganize back four
4. **Switches of Play:** Quick switches to exploit defensive shifts

### Against High Press

1. **GK Distribution:** Use GK as extra player (if comfortable)
2. **Long Balls:** Bypass press with balls to #9 dropping
3. **Third-Man Runs:** Beat press with coordinated runs
4. **Direct Play:** Early passes into final third to exploit space behind

### Against Man-Marking

1. **Create Confusion:** Players exchange positions to disrupt markers
2. **Exploit Space:** Unmarked players find pockets in defensive block
3. **Runs From Deep:** #6 and #8s arrive late into unmarked space
4. **Isolate 1v1:** Drag defenders out to create favorable matchups

## Key Performance Indicators

### Positioning Metrics

- **Zone Occupancy:** Percentage of time spent in assigned zones
- **Spacing:** Average distance between players in possession
- **Line Compactness:** Vertical distance between defensive lines

### Passing Metrics

- **Progressive Passes:** Passes that advance the ball toward goal
- **Passing Combinations:** Length of passing sequences before shot
- **Final Third Entries:** Number of controlled entries vs. long balls

### Pressing Metrics

- **PPDA (Passes Allowed Per Defensive Action):** Lower = more intense press
- **Counter-Press Recovery %:** Possession won back within 5 seconds
- **High Turnovers:** Possession won in opponent's final third

## Training Session Design

### Positional Play Rondos

**4+2 Rondo (20x20m):**
- 4 attackers vs 2 defenders in central area
- 2 neutrals on outside (end-to-end)
- Objective: 10 consecutive passes = point
- Coaching points: Always have two passing options, receive facing forward

**Position Game (30x20m):**
- 5 vs 5 with zones for positional play
- Three horizontal zones
- Must progress through zones to score
- Coaching points: Structural superiority, timing of runs

### Small-Sided Games

**7v7 with Three Zones:**
- Score by dribbling/passing into end zone
- Must use all three zones to score
- Encourages: Positioning, combination play, compact defense
