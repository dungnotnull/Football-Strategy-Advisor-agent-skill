# Training Periodization — Framework Reference

## Core Concepts

Periodization is the systematic planning of athletic training to reach peak performance at the right time. This framework synthesizes principles from Bondarchuk's transfer of training and Matveyev's classical periodization model.

### Periodization Philosophy

**"Train, Don't Strain"**

- Training should stress the body to stimulate adaptation
- Too little stress = no adaptation (plateau)
- Too much stress = injury, burnout, overtraining
- Optimal stress = progressive adaptation (supercompensation)

### The Supercompensation Cycle

```
Fitness Level
     ↑
     |        ___Supercompensation___
     |       /                      \
     |      /                        \
     |  ___/                          \___
     | /   Training Stimulus            \
     |/                                    \
     +--------------------------------------→ Time
        T    Recovery     Adaptation     Ready
```

1. **Training Stimulus:** Fatigue from training
2. **Recovery:** Rest, nutrition, sleep
3. **Supercompensation:** Fitness exceeds previous level
4. **Timing:** Next stimulus during supercompensation window

## Macrocycle Structure

### Seasonal Periodization (11-Month Season)

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                        MACROCYCLE (Season)                          │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│                                                                       │
│  ┌─────────┐   ┌─────────┐   ┌─────────┐   ┌─────────┐   ┌───────┐ │
│  │Pre-Season│  │Compet 1 │  │Compet 2 │  │Compet 3 │  │Recovery│ │
│  │  8 wks   │   │ 12 wks  │   │ 12 wks  │   │ 12 wks  │  │ 4 wks  │ │
│  └─────────┘   └─────────┘   └─────────┘   └─────────┘   └───────┘ │
│                                                                       │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Pre-Season (8 weeks):**
- Focus: Base fitness, strength, tactical foundation
- Volume: High
- Intensity: Medium
- Tactical: Simple patterns, formation basics

**Competitive Phase 1 (12 weeks):**
- Focus: Peak fitness, match readiness, tactical refinement
- Volume: Medium
- Intensity: High
- Tactical: Complex patterns, game-specific situations

**Competitive Phase 2 (12 weeks):**
- Focus: Maintain fitness, tactical flexibility
- Volume: Medium
- Intensity: High (peak)
- Tactical: Set-piece specialization, opponent-specific

**Competitive Phase 3 (12 weeks):**
- Focus: Maintain freshness, tactical execution
- Volume: Low-Medium
- Intensity: High (match-specific)
- Tactical: Repetition of key patterns

**Recovery (4 weeks):**
- Focus: Regeneration, analysis, planning
- Volume: Low
- Intensity: Low
- Tactical: Light review, individual analysis

## Mesocycle Structure

### 4-Week Mesocycle

**Standard Pattern:**
```
Week 1: Load (High volume, Medium intensity)
Week 2: Load (High volume, High intensity)
Week 3: Unload (Medium volume, Medium intensity)
Week 4: Recover (Low volume, Low intensity)
```

**Alternative Patterns:**

**3-Week Mesocycle:**
```
Week 1: Load (High volume, High intensity)
Week 2: Load (Medium volume, High intensity)
Week 3: Recover (Low volume, Low intensity)
```

**5-Week Mesocycle (for long competitive periods):**
```
Week 1: Load (High volume, High intensity)
Week 2: Load (High volume, High intensity)
Week 3: Load (Medium volume, High intensity)
Week 4: Unload (Low volume, Medium intensity)
Week 5: Recover (Low volume, Low intensity)
```

### In-Season Mesocycle (Match Week)

**Typical Week with Saturday Match:**

**Monday:**
- Focus: Recovery (light training, video analysis)
- Intensity: Low
- Duration: 45-60 min

**Tuesday:**
- Focus: Tactical training, medium conditioning
- Intensity: Medium
- Duration: 75-90 min

**Wednesday:**
- Focus: High-intensity conditioning, set-pieces
- Intensity: High
- Duration: 60-75 min

**Thursday:**
- Focus: Tactical preparation for opponent
- Intensity: Medium-High
- Duration: 60-75 min

**Friday:**
- Focus: Light preparation, set-piece rehearsal
- Intensity: Low
- Duration: 45-60 min

**Saturday:**
- Focus: Match
- Intensity: Maximum (match)
- Duration: 90 min

**Sunday:**
- Focus: Rest / Light recovery
- Intensity: Very Low
- Duration: 30-45 min (optional)

## Training Components

### Endurance Training

**Aerobic Capacity (Pre-Season Focus):**
- Duration: 20-40 min continuous
- Intensity: 70-80% max heart rate
- Frequency: 2-3 times/week
- Methods: Long runs, cycling, interval training

**Aerobic Power (In-Season Maintenance):**
- Duration: 4-6 min intervals
- Intensity: 85-90% max heart rate
- Frequency: 1-2 times/week
- Methods: 4x4 min intervals, tempo runs

**Anaerobic Capacity (Match Fitness):**
- Duration: 30 sec - 2 min intervals
- Intensity: 90-100% max heart rate
- Frequency: 1-2 times/week
- Methods: Shuttle runs, sprint intervals, small-sided games

### Strength Training

**Maximal Strength (Pre-Season):**
- Exercises: Squats, deadlifts, bench press
- Reps: 3-6 reps per set
- Sets: 3-5 sets
- Frequency: 2-3 times/week
- Rest: 3-5 min between sets

**Explosive Power (In-Season):**
- Exercises: Plyometrics, Olympic lifts, jumps
- Reps: 3-5 reps per set
- Sets: 3-4 sets
- Frequency: 1-2 times/week
- Rest: 2-3 min between sets

**Strength Endurance (Match Demands):**
- Exercises: Circuit training, bodyweight exercises
- Reps: 12-15 reps per set
- Sets: 2-3 sets
- Frequency: 1-2 times/week
- Rest: 30-60 sec between sets

### Speed Training

**Acceleration (0-10m):**
- Drills: Short sprints from standing, starts
- Reps: 5-10 reps
- Rest: 1-2 min between reps
- Frequency: 1-2 times/week

**Max Speed (10-30m):**
- Drills: Flying sprints, in-and-out runs
- Reps: 3-6 reps
- Rest: 2-3 min between reps
- Frequency: 1 time/week

**Agility (Change of Direction):**
- Drills: Pro agility, T-drill, ladder drills
- Reps: 5-8 reps
- Rest: 1-2 min between reps
- Frequency: 1-2 times/week

### Tactical Training

**Shape Training (Formation & Structure):**
- Focus: Positioning, spacing, movement patterns
- Duration: 20-30 min
- Methods: Shadow play, walk-throughs, zone games

**Pressing Training (Transition):**
- Focus: Pressing triggers, counter-pressing, recovery
- Duration: 15-25 min
- Methods: Rondo-based pressing games, transition games

**Set-Piece Training:**
- Focus: Attacking and defending set-pieces
- Duration: 15-20 min
- Methods: Rehearsed routines, video analysis

**Possession Training:**
- Focus: Passing combinations, positional play
- Duration: 20-30 min
- Methods: Rondo, possession games, positional games

## Training Load Management

### Acute:Chronic Workload Ratio (ACWR)

**Acute Workload:** 7-day training load
**Chronic Workload:** 28-day average training load

**Formula:**
```
ACWR = Acute Workload / Chronic Workload
```

**Interpretation:**
- **ACWR < 0.8:** Undertraining, risk of deconditioning
- **ACWR 0.8-1.3:** Sweet spot, optimal adaptation
- **ACWR 1.3-1.5:** Caution zone, monitor fatigue
- **ACWR > 1.5:** Danger zone, high injury risk

### Training Load Calculation

**Session Rating of Perceived Exertion (sRPE):**
```
Load = Duration (minutes) × Intensity (sRPE 1-10)
```

**Example:**
- 90-minute session at intensity 7
- Load = 90 × 7 = 630 units

**Weekly Load:**
- Sum of all session loads in the week

### Fatigue Monitoring

**Subjective Measures:**
- Morning fatigue (1-10 scale)
- Muscle soreness (1-10 scale)
- Sleep quality (1-10 scale)
- Mood state (1-10 scale)

**Objective Measures:**
- Heart rate variability (HRV)
- Resting heart rate (RHR)
- Performance tests (sprint times, jump height)
- Wellness questionnaire

### Adjusting Training

**When Fatigue is High:**
- Reduce volume (fewer reps, shorter duration)
- Maintain intensity (quality over quantity)
- Focus on recovery
- Consider extra rest day

**When Fatigue is Low:**
- Increase volume gradually (10-20% increase)
- Maintain or slightly increase intensity
- Introduce new challenges
- Monitor response to increases

## Training Session Design

### Tactical Periodization Session

**Topic:** Defensive Shape & Pressing Triggers (4-3-3)

**Warm-up (15 min):**
- Rondo (5 min): 6v2 in 20x20m area
- Activation (10 min): Dynamic movements, sprints

**Phase 1: Defensive Shape (20 min):**
- Organization: 4-4-2 mid-block in 30x40m area
- Rules: Defensive team presses on triggers
- Coaching points: Compactness, lateral shifting, pressing coordination

**Phase 2: Pressing Game (25 min):**
- 8v8 game with pressing triggers
- Score by: 1 point for goal, 3 points for turnover in final third
- Coaching points: Immediate press, coordinated movements, recovery

**Phase 3: Small-Sided Game (20 min):**
- 6v6 with goalkeepers
- Focus: Applying defensive shape in game situation
- Coaching points: Communication, positioning, transitions

**Cool-down (10 min):**
- Stretching, feedback

**Load Calculation:**
- Duration: 90 min
- Intensity: 7 (high)
- Load: 90 × 7 = 630 units

### Match Preparation Session

**Topic:** Opponent: High-Pressing Team (4-3-3)

**Warm-up (15 min):**
- Ball circulation, passive pressing

**Phase 1: Build-Up Play (25 min):**
- Organization: GK + back 4 vs 2 opponents
- Objective: Progress through midfield against high press
- Coaching points: Calmness, passing angles, third-man runs

**Phase 2: Counter-Pressing Game (25 min):**
- 7v7 with counter-pressing emphasis
- Score by: Normal goal (1 point), goal from counter-press (3 points)
- Coaching points: Immediate reaction, coordinated press, recovery

**Phase 3: Set-Piece Rehearsal (15 min):**
- 2 corner routines (attack)
- 2 free kick routines (defense)
- Coaching points: Timing, movement, communication

**Cool-down (10 min):**
- Stretching, match briefing

## Periodization Calendar

### Pre-Season Mesocycle (8 Weeks)

**Week 1-2: Base Fitness**
- Focus: Endurance, strength base
- Volume: High
- Intensity: Medium
- Tactical: Basic shape

**Week 3-4: Build Fitness**
- Focus: Anaerobic capacity, explosive power
- Volume: High
- Intensity: High
- Tactical: Pressing, transitions

**Week 5-6: Peak Fitness**
- Focus: Match intensity, tactical complexity
- Volume: Medium
- Intensity: High
- Tactical: Set-pieces, game situations

**Week 7-8: Match Readiness**
- Focus: Tactical execution, match simulation
- Volume: Medium
- Intensity: High
- Tactical: Full match scenarios

### In-Season Mesocycle (4 Weeks)

**Week 1: Load**
- 3 high-intensity sessions
- 2 tactical sessions
- 1 match
- 1 recovery

**Week 2: Load**
- 3 high-intensity sessions
- 2 tactical sessions
- 1 match
- 1 recovery

**Week 3: Unload**
- 2 medium-intensity sessions
- 2 tactical sessions
- 1 match
- 1 recovery

**Week 4: Recover**
- 1 low-intensity session
- 1 tactical session
- 1 match
- 1 recovery
