## Research Papers & Sources (20)

1. J. Wilson (2008). Inverting the Pyramid: The History of Football Tactics. Orion — Comprehensive tactical-history reference.
2. C. Anderson, D. Sally (2013). The Numbers Game: Why Everything You Know About Football Is Wrong. Penguin — Analytics-driven critique of traditional tactical assumptions.
3. IMPECT/Opta research summarized in W. Spearman et al. (2017). Physics-Based Modeling of Pass Probabilities in Soccer. MIT Sloan Sports Analytics Conference — xG/possession-value modeling foundations.
4. L. Pappalardo et al. (2019). A Public Data Set of Spatio-Temporal Match Events in Soccer Competitions. Scientific Data — Standard dataset/methodology basis for tactical analytics.
5. T. Decroos, L. Bransen, J. Van Haaren, J. Davis (2019). Actions Speak Louder Than Goals: Valuing Player Actions in Soccer. ACM SIGKDD — VAEP possession-value framework.
6. D. Bondarchuk (2007). Transfer of Training in Sports. Ultimate Athlete Concepts — Periodization theory foundation.
7. L. Matveyev (1981). Fundamentals of Sports Training. Progress Publishers — Classical periodization model.
8. J. Guardiola interviews/analyses compiled in M. Perarnau (2014). Pep Confidential. Arena Sport — Positional-play tactical philosophy documentation.
9. R. Rein, D. Memmert (2016). Big Data and Tactical Analysis in Elite Soccer. SpringerPlus — Overview of data-driven tactical analysis methods.
10. P. Bradley et al. (2009). High-Intensity Running in English FA Premier League Soccer Matches. Journal of Sports Sciences — Physical-demands data informing tactical/training design.
11. J. Vogelbein, S. Nopp, D. Hökelmann (2014). Defensive Transition in Soccer: Prevalence of Turnover Zones. Journal of Sports Sciences — Transition-moment tactical analysis.
12. D. Sarmento et al. (2014). Match Analysis in Football: A Systematic Review. Journal of Sports Sciences — Review of match-analysis methodologies.
13. F. M. Clemente, F. M. L. Martins, D. Kalamaras, R. S. Mendes (2015). Network Analysis in Football: Position-Specific Behaviour in Association with Ball Progression. International Journal of Performance Analysis in Sport — Network-analysis approach to tactical structure.
14. C. Carling, A. Bloomfield, L. Nelsen, T. Reilly (2008). The Role of Motion Analysis in Elite Soccer. Sports Medicine — Foundations of positional/physical performance tracking.
15. F. Tenga, U. Kanstad, S. Ronglan, R. Bahr (2010). Developing a New Method for Team Match Performance Analysis in Professional Soccer. International Journal of Performance Analysis in Sport — Tactical performance measurement methodology.
16. J. Bangsbo, F. M. Iaia, P. Krustrup (2007). Metabolic Response and Fatigue in Soccer. International Journal of Sports Physiology and Performance — Physiological basis for training periodization.
17. C. Castellano, D. Casamichana (2015). What are the differences between Small-Sided and Large-Sided Games in Soccer Training? A systematic review. Sportverletzung Sportschaden — Training-design methodology.
18. L. Vilar, D. Araújo, K. Davids, Y. Bar-Yam (2013). Science of Winning Soccer: Emergent Pattern-Forming Dynamics in Association Football. Journal of Systems Science and Complexity — Complex-systems tactical analysis.
19. S. Gréhaigne, D. Bouthier, B. David (1997). Dynamic-System Analysis of Opposing Players' Interactions in Collective Actions of Soccer. Journal of Sports Sciences — Positional-play and opponent-interaction theory.
20. M. Sarmento (2018). Team Tactical Performance in Football: A Systematic Review. International Journal of Performance Analysis in Sport — Modern review of tactical-performance analytics.
